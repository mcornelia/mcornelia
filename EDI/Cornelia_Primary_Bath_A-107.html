▲ NORTH UP
  1. Square footage: ≈170 sf total (155" x 180" L-shape less the 54" x 64" notch): bath ≈126 sf (incl. ≈18 sf shower), closet ≈44 sf.
  2. No wall sconces. East vanity task light comes from the lighted medicine cabinets; west makeup light from the pendants + G1. Recessed lights provide all general light.
  3. Use one fixture family for every downlight and gimbal (same 4" aperture and trim), so the ceiling reads as a calm, uniform grid. All 2700–3000K, CRI 90+, dimmable, damp-rated in the bath.
  4. Gimbals go where light needs to be aimed, not just dropped: at faces (G1), into basins without glare in the mirrors (G2), across the oak slats (G3), and onto wardrobe fronts (G4). Plain downlights everywhere else.
  5. Ventilation (upsized per owner): code minimum for ≈126 sf is ≈130 CFM; design for ≈180–230 CFM combined: shower FL 110–150 CFM (6" duct) + toilet F 50–80 CFM. Both quiet DC motors, ducted to the exterior, humidity-sensing with run-on timers. Make-up air comes through the door undercut and the open screen.
  6. Fixture locations are design intent; electrician to adjust a few inches to clear joists, keep G1/G2 positions relative to the faces/basins they serve, and confirm all code requirements (GFCI, wet-location listing over the shower, AFCI).
