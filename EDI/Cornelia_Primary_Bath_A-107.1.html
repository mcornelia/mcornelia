SWITCHING DIAGRAM — WHAT EACH SWITCH CONTROLS

Two stacked 3-gang plates (Legrand adorne, Magnesium) on the ~11" of wall between the 78" double vanity and the bathroom door, upper plate centered ~41" AFF (hand towel hook above at ~61"). Colors on the plan match the switches.

Bank 1 (upper plate) — LIGHTS · all dimmers

1 · General5 ceiling downlights: room, shower entry and the area in front of the screen
Zone Z1
2 · Vanity2 gimbals over the sinks + the Burning Man pendant between the cabinets
Zone Z2 · cabinets dim at their own touch switch
3 · Accent / evening2 gimbals grazing the oak screen + the lighted shower niche
Zone Z4

Bank 2 (lower plate) — SHOWER & FANS

4 · Shower lightLight in the shower fan/light combo
Zone Z5
5 · Shower fanExhaust in the same unit, on its own plain on/off switch (no timer or humidity sensor)
Zone Z5
6 · Toilet fanSeparate fan over the toilet, on a timer
Zone Z6

Controlled elsewhere (not at the door)

Makeup wall, under the mirror (~36"): 2 dimmers: the 2 globe pendants, and the makeup fill gimbal (G1). An outlet sits beside them.
Closet, on the wing wall by the full-length mirror: 1 dimmer for the 4 closet gimbals (Zone Z7, White plate).
Medicine cabinets: on constant power, so their lights run from the touch switch on each cabinet and the inside outlets are always live.
Floor heat: its own thermostat at 52" by the vanity. Niche LED rides with Accent (switch 3).
Still open (yours to decide): whether the vanity pendant gets its own dimmer so it can be a soft night light. Bank 1 is full, so it would need a two-channel dimmer: with the 78" vanity (rev 5b), the ~11" wall beside the door has no room for a 4-gang plate.
