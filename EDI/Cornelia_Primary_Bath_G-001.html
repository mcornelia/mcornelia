PRIMARY BATH & WALK-IN CLOSET — SCOPE OF WORK
Summary for contractor walkthrough and pricing, 10.26.2026. The sheets govern; this page lists the work by trade so nothing is missed. Field-verify everything. Not for permit or construction.

WHO SUPPLIES WHAT

  • Owner purchases and has on site: all finish materials, fixtures and fittings: tile, shower valve, controls, heads and drain, toilet, faucets, medicine cabinets, light fixtures and lamps, fans, wiring devices and plates, accessories and hardware, door lever and hinges, towel warmer, mirrors, glass clamps, closet flooring, IKEA PAX, paint. See owner's order tracker.
  • Contractor/trades supply building materials: framing lumber, blocking, headers, drywall, cement board/backer, caulk/sealant, wire, boxes, breakers, pipe, fittings, vent/duct, fasteners.
  • Tile setter supplies: waterproofing; all floor-heat materials (mat/cable, thermostat, sensor); shower niche LED kit; all Schluter edge and transition profiles (matte black); thinset; grout (owner picks the colors); spacers and leveling system.
  • Separate contracts (by owner): DraytonWorks (Greg): walnut floating vanity, two recessed jog-wall cabinets, slatted oak screen. Modern Concrete of Athens: vanity counter with two integrated sinks + floating 30" makeup counter. Coordinate blocking, power and plumbing locations with both.
  • Owner inspects all finishes on delivery.

1 · DEMOLITION & PREP

  • Remove existing bath and closet finishes, fixtures, cabinetry, flooring and drywall as needed; protect adjacent rooms and the path out; haul-off.
  • Remove the existing 6" square shower drain (replaced by a linear drain).
  • Open walls early where existing conditions drive price (see Field-Verify box).

2 · FRAMING & CARPENTRY

  • Bath door widened 32" → 36", new header (confirm bearing); 36" solid slab, hinged on the south jamb, swings in; slim casing (~2½") on the switch side. A-101/A-102.
  • Recessed openings: two medicine cabinets (~28½"W x 34½"H x 5"D each, centered on the sinks; deepen framing if they're to sit flush), west jog cabinet (~17½" deep, into the cavity behind the shower), east jog cabinet (replaces the existing linen cabinet). A-102/A-106.
  • Shower niche 42" x 12" x ~3¾", west wall, sill ~48" on a tile joint. A-103.
  • Blocking before board: vanity, makeup counter, grab-bar bands (30"–40" all shower walls and beside toilet; full height at shower entry; 15"–40" seat zone), TP holder in the 4" shower return, 4 hooks, towel warmer brackets, magnifying mirror, closet mirror, medicine cabinets. Photograph before closing.
  • Closet wing walls (drywall both faces); install door, casing, baseboard (match house profile).
  • Install owner items: hardware, accessories, mirrors, towel warmer; IKEA PAX assembly/anchoring (confirm who).

3 · PLUMBING

  • Kohler K-39014 valve: set depth to the FINISHED TILE FACE. No extension kit exists. Tile setter gives the build-up; plumber and tile setter check together before board. A-103.
  • Shower: Anthem 3-outlet panel at 54"; wall head ~80"; handheld holder 54" with elbow at 44"; 12" Halo rain head on a 6" ceiling arm (~92"). ¾" supply to the valve; check water pressure; confirm rain-head flow with the inspector.
  • Linear drain at the back wall, floor in one plane; curbless entry.
  • Toilet re-centered: centerline ~16¾" from the shower return and from the vanity (15" min. code); 12" rough-in to the finished wall. Relocate the drain if needed. Bidet toilet needs power (see Electrical).
  • Vanity: supplies and drains up through the floor; confirm vent route clear of the medicine cabinet openings. Two 1-hole KANE faucets; match the guest bath faucet-to-wall setback.

4 · ELECTRICAL, LIGHTING & EXHAUST

  • Per A-107: 5 wafer downlights, 9 square gimbals (bath + closet), 2 makeup-wall pendants, 1 vanity pendant (PV), feeds to 2 lighted medicine cabinets, niche LED with remote driver, optional toe-kick LED.
  • Exhaust: shower fan/light 110–150 CFM (6" duct), toilet fan 50–80 CFM; both ducted outside, humidity/timer control.
  • Switches: two stacked 3-gang plates by the door (S1 lights, S2 shower & fans); makeup wall 2-gang + 1-gang at ~36". Vanity pendant separate dimming: owner deciding.
  • Receptacles (all GFCI-protected): 3 at the vanity ~41", makeup outlet, bidet outlet behind toilet, towel warmer outlet ~6", 2 in the closet.
  • Floor heat under all bath tile; thermostat on the north wall at 52"; dedicated circuit as required.

5 · WATERPROOFING, TILE & GLASS

  • Waterproof shower walls, floor and niche; curbless transition.
  • Floor: Stonehenge 24x24 throughout the bath; Sahara 4x4 in the shower. Walls: Stonehenge floor-to-ceiling in the shower; elsewhere Stonehenge wainscot (2 tiles, ~47") + Sahara 3x12 kit-kat vertical to the ceiling, flush transition. Penny round at the makeup wall and niche back.
  • Joints: 1/16" rectified (leveling system), 1/8" kit-kat. Matte black Schluter at exposed edges and the flush tile/oak seam; door threshold transition.
  • Shower glass: fixed floor-to-ceiling ⅜" low-iron panel, owner's unlacquered brass clamps; template after tile.

6 · FLOORING, DRYWALL & PAINT

  • Closet: owner's Stuga white oak + underlayment; flush seam to the tile.
  • Drywall: closet, wing walls, ceilings.
  • Paint (BM Simply White): all ceilings Aura Bath & Spa matte; closet + wing walls eggshell; baseboards satin; door casing SW Tricorn Black satin.

FIELD-VERIFY (AFFECTS PRICE)

  • Door header size / whether the wall is bearing.
  • Existing toilet drain and vanity drain/vent locations.
  • Wall depths at the medicine cabinets and jog cabinets; any pipes, vents or wiring in those bays.
  • Ceiling height and joist direction for the rain-head arm, fans, pendants and duct runs.
  • Electrical panel capacity for floor heat, bidet toilet and new circuits.
  • Water pressure at the shower.

OPEN ITEMS (OWNER)

  • Medicine cabinets: confirm Fresca light color (~3000K) and a left/right pair.
  • Vanity pendant on its own dimmer or shared.
  • Greg's estimate (vanity, jog cabinets, screen); counter color (black vs. charcoal).
  • Grout colors (tile setter purchases once chosen).
