DIMENSIONS TO VERIFY

The original design brief specified plan (footprint) dimensions in detail, but did not specify most vertical/mounting heights. The heights below are reasonable industry-standard assumptions used to draft this elevation — none are confirmed selections. Confirm each with the relevant trade before fabrication/install:

  1. Vanity counter height (assumed 34" AFF) — confirm w/ DraytonWorks (Greg) and Modern Concrete of Athens.
  2. Cabinet float height (assumed 6" AFF at underside) — confirm w/ DraytonWorks; affects blocking height per Note 5 on A-101.
  3. Backsplash — ELIMINATED by owner 2026-10-06. Counter is one Black Concrete slab with two integrated sinks; it meets the Stonehenge wainscot directly (color-matched silicone joint). Same on the west makeup countertop (A-104).
  4. Lighted medicine cabinets — owner 2026-10-07: two lighted mirrored medicine cabinets side by side, recessed if at all possible, frameless, straight edges; vertical light bands in the mirror face on both sides of each unit. They serve as the east vanity lighting. Drawn 2 x 40"W x 30"H, 49"–79" AFF (e.g., Kohler Verdera lighted 40x30; Robern also under consideration). Specs to look for: CRI 90+, ~3000K, dimmable, defog. Interior outlet (and USB) REQUIRED (owner 2026-10-07) so electric toothbrushes and a water flosser charge out of sight; confirm interior depth fits them. Before framing, confirm: (a) stud depth vs. the unit's recess depth (2x4 wall may need furring or 2x6); (b) sink supply and drain lines come up through the floor (owner 2026-10-07), so they don't conflict with the openings; plumber to confirm the vent route (e.g., under the floor or an air admittance valve in the vanity) also stays clear; (c) headers for two ~40" rough openings; (d) electrical to each unit.
  5. Wainscot + kit-kat — CONFIRMED 2026-10-06: Stonehenge Earth 24x24, two full tiles high (0–~47" AFF incl. grout, no cut at top); Sahara 3x12 Dark, vertical stack, 47" AFF to ceiling; flush transition, no trim; partial kit-kat row at the ceiling. Bath portion of the wall only (north of the closet screen line).
  6. Faucets — Rejuvenation Monterey single-hole, Antique Brass, CONFIRMED (same faucet already performs well with an integrated concrete sink in the guest suite). Match the guest bath's faucet-to-wall setback.
  7. Outlets — 3 Legrand adorne outlets in Magnesium at ~41" AFF in the terrazzo band: left = GFCI (AGFTR2152M4, power enters here), center = 65W USB-C plus-size outlet (ARTRUSB15PD65M4), right = regular (ARCD152M10); center and right wired on the GFCI load side. Keep each box within a single tile; do not straddle the 47" wainscot line.
  8. East vanity lighting — provided by the lighted medicine cabinets (no separate sconces).
  9. Floor heat thermostat — REV 3c (10.08.26, owner): on the north wall (perpendicular to this elevation, at its left end), above the north end of the vanity, ~10-12" from the corner, 52" AFF to center in the kit-kat. Hinge the north medicine cabinet on its south edge so its door clears the thermostat. See A-101.
  10. Door width / swing — REV 3: widened to 36" (was 32"), solid door per owner; hinged on the south jamb, swings in toward the closet (rev 3b). Switch bank (two 3-gang Magnesium plates, stacked) on the 10" wall north of the door, so keep that casing slim; opening extends north toward the vanity, leaving ~10" of wall. New header required — GC to confirm framing.
  11. Door height (assumed 80" standard) — not specified in original brief; verify against actual door unit.
