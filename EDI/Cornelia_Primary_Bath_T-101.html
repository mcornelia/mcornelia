ABOUT THIS SET
  • Purpose: walkthrough, scope review and pricing with the general contractor. Preliminary design intent, not for permit or construction.
  • Rev 5 (10.31.26): plans redrawn to the owners' field measurements (shower 35" x 76", east vanity 78" x 22", makeup wall 50", closet 46" deep). Existing cabinets and the drop-in shower limit access, so all dimensions verify in field after demolition.
  • Owner-furnished: the owners purchase and stage all finish materials, fixtures, lighting, devices and hardware (quantities on A-108 / A-109). The GC supplies building materials; the tile setter supplies waterproofing, floor heat, Schluter profiles, grout and setting materials (see G-001).
  • Separate owner contracts: DraytonWorks (walnut vanity, jog-wall cabinets, slatted screen); Modern Concrete of Athens (vanity and makeup counters).
  • Start with G-001 for scope by trade and the field-verify list. A-108 / A-109 is the finish schedule, with product links.
