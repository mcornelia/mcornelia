The Lab

Practical AI, small radios, useful tools, and games built for fun.

This is my workbench on the internet—a place for projects that begin with a real problem, a curious question, or a joke that gets slightly out of hand.

Some become tools we use every day. Others simply teach me something new. I share the useful parts so other people can try them too.

// on the bench

  • AI agents — experiments built with both Codex and OpenClaw, from focused coding partners to always-on household assistants, family messaging, and model routing
  • Home systems — a whole-house maintenance tracker, morning briefs, reminders, and small automations running from a dedicated Mac mini
  • Meshtastic & LoRa — experiments with small radios, long-range messaging, and communication that doesn’t depend on cellular service or the internet
  • The Daily Fetch — a daily word puzzle made for Aimee, starring Olive and her orange-and-blue ball. Huck supervises.
  • Retro Arcade — browser-sized versions of Asteroids, Snake, Breakout, Pong, Frogger, and a data-center-themed Space Invaders
  • Useful little tools — a room-layout planner, setup guides, monitoring jobs, and whatever problem looks interesting next

// how things get built

The process is deliberately practical: start with a problem, build the smallest useful version, test it in real life, and improve whatever somebody trips over. I use AI as a collaborator throughout and publish the pieces that might help someone else.

// outside the lab

The lab is in Athens, Georgia, where I live with my partner Aimee and our dogs, Olive and Huck. Away from the keyboard: woodworking, cooking food that’s probably too spicy, and games with friends.
