The Lab

Practical AI, small radios, useful tools, and games built for fun.

This is my workbench on the internet — a place for projects that begin with a real problem, a curious question, or a joke that gets slightly out of hand.

Some become tools we use every day. Others simply teach me something new. I share the useful parts so other people can try them too.

// how things get built

The process is deliberately practical: start with a problem, build the smallest useful version, test it in real life, and improve whatever somebody trips over. I use AI as a collaborator throughout and publish the pieces that might help someone else.

// outside the lab

The lab is in Athens, Georgia, where I live with my partner Aimee and our dogs, Olive and Huck. Away from the keyboard: woodworking, cooking food that’s probably too spicy, and games with friends.
