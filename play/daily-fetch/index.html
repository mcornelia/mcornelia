The search guide

How to fetch—and win

  1. 1

    Follow the scent. Connect touching letters across, down, or diagonally. Drag and release, or tap letters and press Fetch It. A tile can only be used once.

  2. 2

    Fetch real words. Words need at least three letters and cannot be repeated. Huck handles inspection.

  3. 3

    Build your score. Three-letter words earn 1 point; then 2, 3, 5, 7, and 10+ points as words get longer. The score is just for fun.

  4. 4

    Find eight words total. One of those eight must be the hidden ball word. The ball word is always 6+ letters, so it also completes the long throw. You do not need any additional words.
