RHC-027 reviewed

Carved Three-Dimensional Wolf Pup Family Group, Heart-Shaped Base, on Wood Stand

Object Type
Fully carved (not flat-engraved) three-dimensional sculpture in the round, depicting three nestled wolf/fox pups within a heart-shaped silhouette base; mounted on a dark wood display base.
Material
Presumed ivory or bone (not resin/composite as initially suspected) — the underside of the piece (RHC-027-e), visible where it separates from the wood base, shows a raw, unfinished natural surface with cream-tan mottling, fine fissures, and organic color variation entirely consistent with genuine tooth or tusk material rather than cast resin.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 10.0cm, width: 9.0cm, height: 6.5cm — Approximate footprint (~10.0 x 9.0cm) and height (~6.5cm) estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos; this is a three-dimensional object, so a single length/width pair only partially describes its form.
Subject Matter
Three wolf or fox pups nestled together in a tight, curled group, their bodies and tails forming an overall heart-shaped silhouette. Fully sculptural (carved in the round) rather than a flat engraved scene — the first such piece in the collection.
Inscriptions
  • “Faint scratched mark, possibly initials above a four-digit number (tentatively read as a year, '1988'?)” (on the raw underside of the piece, near the edge, partially obscured by the item tag in photos)
    Not confidently legible after several zoom/crop attempts; underside is naturally mottled and the mark is very shallow. Flagged for physical re-inspection rather than continued digital cropping.

Mount / Display Stand
Mounted on a dark wood base shaped to match/support the heart-form silhouette of the carving.
Color / Technique
Fully carved sculptural form; natural material coloring only (cream to tan), no ink line engraving or color washes — a fundamentally different technique from every other piece cataloged so far, which are all flat-engraved scrimshaw.
Condition Notes
Sculpture appears intact with natural surface mottling throughout; underside shows expected raw/unfinished texture where it was carved from the original block. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
The first fully three-dimensional, carved-in-the-round sculpture in the collection — every other item so far is a flat engraved scene on a tooth, plaque, panel, or tool. This raises a cataloging question of whether it belongs in the same 'scrimshaw' category at all, since scrimshaw traditionally refers to engraved/incised line work rather than sculptural carving; worth flagging for the collection-naming discussion.

Material not confirmed (presumed ivory/bone based on visual texture, not resin). If ivory, age and species would need confirmation before any loan/sale. Flag for legal review; not a legal determination.
