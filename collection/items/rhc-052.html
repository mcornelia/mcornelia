Carved Ivory Mounted Warrior/Huntsman on Rearing Horse, with Separate Scroll-Carved Rectangular Plinth (Matched to RHC-050)

Object Type
A fully carved (not flat-engraved) sculptural group of a mounted figure on a rearing horse, raising a weapon overhead, shown together with a separate rectangular carved plinth/box element with scrolling foliate relief — nearly identical in subject, pose, scale, and companion plinth to RHC-050, strongly indicating the two form a matched pair or set.
Material
Presumed ivory (cream-white, finely detailed carving with light natural mottling).
Dimensions (cm)
length: 9.0cm — Rider/horse group ~9.0cm long, closely matching RHC-050's dimensions; estimated off the cm scale shown in photos b and c.
Subject Matter
A robed, bearded rider mounted on a rearing horse, one arm raised overhead brandishing a curved weapon, the horse shown mid-rear with front legs raised and head turned — essentially the same dynamic hunting/battle composition as RHC-050, shown alongside a matching rectangular plinth with scrolling acanthus/foliate relief.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: South/Southeast Asian carved ivory, possibly Indian or Persian-influenced equestrian hunting scene)
Mount / Display Stand
Uncertain — photographed adjacent to a separate rectangular carved plinth/box element matching RHC-050's; not clear whether the rider group originally sat atop this plinth or whether it is a separate accompanying piece.
Color / Technique
Fully carved sculptural form with fine detailed texture (horse mane/tail, rider's robe folds, harness patterning) matching RHC-050's technique; natural material coloring only, no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Figural group appears intact with fine, delicate elements (raised weapon arm, horse legs) preserved with no visible breaks; the rectangular plinth also appears intact. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Near-identical companion to RHC-050 in subject, pose, scale, and accompanying plinth — the two almost certainly form a matched pair or set of equestrian hunting-scene sculptures.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory, species and import history would need confirmation before any loan/sale (CITES/ESA considerations). Flag for legal review; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No attribution identified; copyright status undetermined.

Open Research Questions

Research finding (phase 3, 2026-07-15): See RHC-050 — same finding applies (Qing dynasty Chinese carved ivory warrior-on-horse figures are a documented collector category); this pair's shared construction and matching plinths support them being produced together rather than one being a later addition, though only physical inspection of the plinth-to-figure mortise fit (per the original research note) can confirm whether the bases are original.
