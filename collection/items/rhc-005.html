Engraved Bison Scene Piece on Rod-and-Base Display Mount

Object Type
Small engraved oval piece (tooth or carved material), displayed on a specimen-style mount — a metal rod set into a small round wood base, with the piece fixed atop the rod
Material
Base material of the engraved piece presumed bone, antler, or tooth (not confirmed) — glossy surface with a natural or applied amber-to-caramel color gradient. Mount is a brass or similar metal rod set into a light-colored wood disk base.

Open Research Questions

Research finding (phase 3, 2026-07-15): Rod-and-base display mounts of this kind were a common commercial fitting sold by scrimshaw/dealer suppliers rather than bespoke woodworking — worth comparing directly against RHC-006 through RHC-011's similar rod-and-base mounts (per the original note) to see if several pieces share a common supplier, though no specific manufacturer was identified.
