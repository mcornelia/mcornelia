Open Research Questions

Research finding (phase 3, 2026-07-15): General web search for small turned ivory finials/chess pieces of this general form didn't narrow the function further than the original note already outlines (chess piece vs. finial vs. fragment) — this is a physical-examination question (checking for a matching set, wear pattern, or attachment point) rather than one a web search can resolve. Per Richard H. Cornelia (written review of printed catalog booklet, 2026-07-19): Richard H. Cornelia's written review states as a collection-wide fact: "There are no bone items; bone is clearly characterized by dark, black flecks of dried blood in the pores of the item. I always steered clear of bone; concentrating on ivory instead." Applied here to resolve this item's previous ivory-or-bone hedge in favor of ivory. Additionally, on long undecorated items like this he noted: "They are all ivory, but I'd need to re-examine them to determine species, elephant, mammoth or walrus which I think is not too important. Identifying them as ivory is sufficient."
