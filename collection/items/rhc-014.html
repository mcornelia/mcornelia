RHC-014 reviewed

Engraved Dinosaur Plaque on Natural-Edge Ivory Slab, Signed/Stamped 'Made in Russia, Pohilnyk' (?), on Wood Base

Object Type
Engraved flat slab/plaque with an irregular natural edge, mounted upright on a wood base
Material
Likely mammoth ivory rather than sperm whale tooth — the piece is a flat slab with an irregular, bark-like natural edge (not a tooth silhouette), and the reverse bears an incised maker's mark reading 'Made in Russia' plus an apparent name. Modern mammoth ivory carving is a well-known Russian cottage industry (legal alternative to whale/elephant ivory since mammoths are extinct and unregulated under CITES/MMPA), which fits both the material shape and the mark. Flagged for expert confirmation rather than assumed.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 14.5cm — Length (~14.5cm) estimated off the cm scale; width/thickness not separately measured — ruler only runs along one axis in these photos.
Subject Matter
A theropod dinosaur (T-Rex-like, bipedal, walking pose) engraved in black ink line work with light cross-hatched shading — a modern/novelty subject with no historical whaling-era precedent, consistent with a contemporary decorative piece rather than an antique.
Inscriptions
  • “Made in Russia / Pohilnyk (best-effort reading, uncertain spelling)” (incised on the reverse (unengraved) face of the slab, lower-left area)
    Two lines of cursive-style incised text: 'Made in Russia' is clearly legible; the second line appears to be a name, read tentatively as 'Pohilnyk' or similar Slavic surname (letters partially indistinct) — likely the maker's signature/stamp rather than a historical scrimshander's mark.
Artist Attribution
Possibly 'Pohilnyk' (tentative reading, unconfirmed) — modern maker, not a historical scrimshaw artist
Date / Period
Modern (contemporary/late 20th-21st c.) based on the 'Made in Russia' mark and dinosaur subject matter — clearly not an antique whaling-era piece, distinct from most of the collection.
Mount / Display Stand
Mounted upright on a plain wood base/stand at the slab's wider end.
Color / Technique
Black ink line scrimshaw-style engraving with light cross-hatched shading (no polychrome color)
Condition Notes
Irregular natural edges appear to be an intentional design feature (retaining the material's organic outline) rather than damage. Surface otherwise appears intact. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First confirmed modern/non-antique piece in the collection, evidenced by the 'Made in Russia' maker's mark and the dinosaur subject. Flat natural-edge slab form is distinct from the tooth-silhouette pieces seen elsewhere. Important to keep clearly distinguished from the antique whaling-era items when the collection is described or valued.
Legal / Regulatory Status
If confirmed as mammoth ivory (extinct species), this piece falls outside MMPA/CITES/ESA restrictions that apply to sperm whale, elephant, or other extant-species ivory — but this is not confirmed and should be verified before any loan/sale; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
Maker's mark present (tentatively 'Pohilnyk') — underlying image likely remains under the maker's copyright, independent of physical ownership; not confirmed.
