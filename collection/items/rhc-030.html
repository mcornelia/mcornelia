RHC-030 reviewed

Carved Bear Family Sculpture, Adult Bear with Two Climbing Cubs on Rocky Outcrop

Object Type
Fully carved (not flat-engraved) three-dimensional sculpture depicting an adult bear with two smaller cubs, set against a textured rocky/tree-like backdrop element. Appears unmounted (no wood base or metal pin visible in either photo) — possibly a fragment broken free of an original base, or intended to be freestanding/hand-held.
Material
Presumed ivory or bone (cream-tan coloring and fine surface texture consistent with RHC-027, RHC-028, RHC-029). A small area of reddish-brown staining is visible at the top edge of the rocky backdrop element (RHC-030-a.jpg) — could be natural material variation, old adhesive/mount residue, or surface staining; not confidently identified from photos.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 6.3cm — Overall span (~6.3cm) estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos; a small three-dimensional group, comparable in size to RHC-028 and RHC-029.
Subject Matter
A large adult bear on all fours in the foreground, with two smaller bear cubs standing upright on their hind legs behind/beside it, appears to be climbing or reaching up a textured rocky outcrop or tree-stump form that rises behind the group. A family/genre wildlife scene rendered fully in the round, in the same sculptural style as RHC-027 (wolf pups) and RHC-028 (beluga whale).
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated; no signature or mark found
Mount / Display Stand
None visible in either photo — the piece appears to be unmounted, resting directly on the photo background. A small fleck of green material is visible at the base in RHC-030-b.jpg, possibly adhesive or mount residue, suggesting it may originally have been affixed to a base that is now missing.
Color / Technique
Fully carved sculptural form with individually carved fur/body detail on the bears and a distinct textured (bark- or rock-like) surface on the backdrop element; natural material coloring only, no ink line work or paint.
Condition Notes
Surface appears intact with no obvious breaks across the main figures; the backdrop/rock element has an irregular, possibly broken top edge, and no base or mount is present, raising the possibility this piece is incomplete (missing a base) or was separated from a larger mount. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Fourth fully three-dimensional carved sculpture in the collection (after RHC-027, RHC-028, RHC-029), and the only one so far with no visible mount, base, or pin — worth flagging for physical inspection to determine if a base is genuinely missing. Continues the sculptural genre/family-scene tradition seen in RHC-027 (wolf pups).

Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory/bone, species and age would need confirmation before any loan/sale. Flag for legal review and material testing; not a legal determination.
