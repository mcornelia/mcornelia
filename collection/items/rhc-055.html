Elaborately Carved Ceremonial Elephant with Multi-Tiered Spired Howdah, on Red Velvet-Mounted Base (Dealer Tag: "Item 6, 140gm, 2½")

Object Type
A densely and elaborately carved ivory figure of a caparisoned ceremonial elephant bearing a multi-tiered, pierced/openwork spired howdah (canopy structure) on its back, mounted on an oval base affixed to a red velvet pad; the base bears an old dealer/collector paper tag reading 'Item 6,' '140gm,' and '2½,' alongside a small printed elephant silhouette logo.
Material
Presumed ivory (cream-white, densely detailed carving throughout), mounted on a red velvet-covered base.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 10.0cm — Overall length ~10.0cm estimated off the cm scale shown in photo b; old dealer tag additionally records a weight of 140g and a dimension of 2½ (units not specified, likely inches, possibly height).
Subject Matter
A ceremonially dressed elephant, its body covered in a densely patterned caparison/blanket with floral roundel medallions and fringed borders, bearing a tall multi-tiered spired howdah or canopy structure with pierced/openwork side panels and multiple finial spires — a highly elaborate ceremonial procession elephant, continuing and culminating the elephant motif seen earlier in RHC-042 and RHC-045.
Inscriptions
  • “Item 6" / "140gm" / "2½" (handwritten in cursive ink) alongside a small printed black elephant silhouette logo” (Paper tag affixed to the red velvet base)
    Old dealer or collector inventory tag recording an item number, weight (140 grams), and a dimension (2½, units unspecified); the printed elephant logo may indicate a specific ivory dealer's branding. Useful provenance evidence — not yet researched.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: South/Southeast Asian, likely Indian, carved ivory ceremonial elephant)
Mount / Display Stand
Mounted on an oval base affixed to a red velvet-covered pad, which also carries the old dealer/collector paper tag.
Color / Technique
Extremely dense, multi-layered relief and pierced/openwork carving covering nearly the entire surface (caparison patterning, howdah lattice work, finial spires); natural material coloring only, no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Piece appears intact with fine, delicate openwork spires and lattice elements preserved across all 6 photos; no visible losses. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Most elaborate and densely carved of the collection's three elephant-motif pieces (with RHC-042's hairpins and RHC-045's simpler figure), serving as a fitting capstone piece; retains an old dealer/collector tag with weight and item-number data, a rare piece of physical provenance evidence not present on other items in the collection.
Provenance Notes
Old paper tag on the base reads 'Item 6, 140gm, 2½' with a printed elephant logo — likely from a prior dealer or auction lot; origin of the tag not yet researched.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory, species and import history would need confirmation before any loan/sale (CITES/ESA considerations for Asian elephant ivory in particular). Flag for legal review; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No attribution identified; copyright status undetermined.
