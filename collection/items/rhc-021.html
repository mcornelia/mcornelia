RHC-021 reviewed

Polychrome Engraved Bald Eagle Catching Fish, Signed Paul Logan, on Fossil Walrus Ivory, in Wood Slot Stand

Object Type
Engraved natural-form panel (irregular top edge following the tusk's original contour), displayed upright in a rounded wood block stand with a slot
Material
Fossil walrus ivory — confirmed by a printed foil seal on the reverse reading 'FOSSIL WALRUS IVORY / ESKIMO ARTIFACT.' The reverse also shows the tusk's natural longitudinally-split, weathered root structure, consistent with an unworked/uncarved back face.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 15.0cm, width: 6.0cm — Length (~15.0cm) and width (~6.0cm) estimated off the two cm scales shown in the photos.
Subject Matter
A bald eagle diving with wings swept back, talons extended toward a leaping fish just above the surface of a lake, rendered in fine black ink line work with cross-hatched shading and blue color-wash accents in the water. A tree-lined shore and reflection are visible in the background, giving the scene a naturalistic wilderness/wildlife-art composition distinct from the whaling and maritime scenes elsewhere in the collection.
Inscriptions
  • “Paul Logan” (engraved in a small rectangular signature box at the lower right of the scene)
    Clear, legible cursive signature, printed/engraved within a small bordered box — a presentation style suggesting a produced/edition piece rather than a one-off hand signature.
  • “FOSSIL WALRUS IVORY / ESKIMO ARTIFACT” (printed foil seal affixed to the reverse (unworked) face)
    Manufacturer/authenticity seal confirming material and describing the piece as an 'Eskimo artifact' — likely a retail/marketing label rather than a maker's mark; worth noting the term 'Eskimo' reflects period marketing language and may warrant more culturally specific terminology (e.g. Alaska Native / Iñupiat / Yupik) in future public-facing description.
Artist Attribution
Paul Logan
Date / Period
Undated; no year visible in signature or seal.
Mount / Display Stand
Displayed upright in a rounded, bookend-style reddish-brown wood block stand with a fitted slot that grips the base of the panel — a mount style not seen elsewhere in the collection so far.
Color / Technique
Black ink line scrimshaw engraving with cross-hatched shading and blue color-wash accents in the water (polychrome, but with a limited, naturalistic palette compared to the more vividly multi-colored pieces like RHC-004/012/013/017)
Condition Notes
Engraved face appears clean and intact with minor natural cracking near the upper edge; reverse shows expected natural fissures and weathering consistent with an uncarved tusk root section. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First piece in the collection with a confirmed material ID via a printed 'FOSSIL WALRUS IVORY' authenticity seal, and the first bald eagle/raptor wildlife subject. Signature is presented in a printed signature-box format rather than a freehand mark, suggesting Paul Logan may be a commercial/production scrimshaw artist rather than a folk-art hobbyist.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Confirmed fossil walrus ivory — generally exempt from MMPA/ESA/CITES restrictions that apply to modern (non-fossil) walrus ivory, since it derives from ancient/archaeological material rather than a recently harvested animal, but state-level rules can vary. Not a legal determination; flag for legal review before any loan/sale.
Rights / Copyright
Signed 'Paul Logan' — underlying image likely remains under the artist's or producer's copyright, independent of physical ownership.
