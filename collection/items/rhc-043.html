RHC-043 reviewed

Carved Woolly Mammoth Sculpture with Separate Oval Base

Object Type
Fully carved (not flat-engraved) three-dimensional sculpture of a woolly mammoth with long curving tusks, paired with a separate small oval base plate that the figure rests on (not permanently affixed — the two pieces are shown separated in RHC-043-b and RHC-043-c).
Material
Presumed ivory (fine detailed fur/wrinkle texture, cream-white with light tan mottling) — notably, mammoth ivory would be a thematically fitting material for this subject, though not confirmed from photos.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 9.0cm — Mammoth figure body ~9.0cm long (tusks extend further forward); separate oval base ~4.0 x 3.0cm; estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos.
Subject Matter
A woolly mammoth in a standing pose, finely carved with individually textured shaggy fur/hair detail covering the body, a raised trunk, small ears, and a pair of long, gracefully curving tusks crossing at the tips — a naturalistic, high-detail sculptural rendering.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated
Mount / Display Stand
Rests on (but is not permanently affixed to) a separate small oval base; the base's top surface shows a faint, roughly butterfly/moth-shaped worn or stained area where the figure's feet contact it, likely from wear, old adhesive, or discoloration rather than a decorative inlay.
Color / Technique
Fully carved sculptural form with fine individually-carved fur/hair texture throughout; natural material coloring only (cream-white with tan mottling), no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Figure appears intact with no visible chips to the fine tusks or legs; the base shows the wear/staining pattern noted above but no structural damage. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Sixth fully three-dimensional carved sculpture in the collection (after RHC-027 through RHC-031), the first depicting an extinct/prehistoric animal, and the first sculpture with a separate (non-permanently-mounted) base rather than a fixed wood stand or metal pin mount.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If mammoth (fossil) ivory, generally exempt from modern ivory trade restrictions as an extinct species; if a different ivory, species and age would need confirmation before any loan/sale. Flag for legal review and material testing; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No attribution identified; copyright status undetermined.

Open Research Questions

Research finding (phase 3, 2026-07-15): Woolly mammoth ivory carvings are commonly produced in the same modern Russian (Yakutia) mammoth-ivory carving industry noted for RHC-014 — worth comparing this piece's carving style/finish against RHC-014 to see if they share a common source, though no direct maker match was found for either.
