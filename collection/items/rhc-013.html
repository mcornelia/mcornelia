Polychrome Engraved Tooth, Two Squirrels Among Flowers and Foliage, on Round Wood Base

Object Type
Engraved tooth, mounted on a round wood display base via a short peg
Material
Presumed tooth (elongated, gently curved conical form), though the overall golden-tan coloration is noticeably deeper/more uniform than the whiter cream teeth elsewhere in the collection (e.g. RHC-007, RHC-010, RHC-011) — could reflect heavier natural aging/staining of the same tooth material, or could indicate a different material; flagged for closer material comparison rather than assumed. Base is a plain, lighter-toned turned wood disc (untinted/lightly finished, distinct from the dark-stained bases used on RHC-007 through RHC-012).
Dimensions (cm)
length: 11.5cm — Length (~11.5cm) estimated off the cm scale; width and base dimensions not separately measured — ruler only runs along one axis in these photos.
Subject Matter
Two squirrels or chipmunks in a naturalistic woodland setting with red flowers/berries and green foliage, rendered in polychrome (black ink line work with hand-applied color washes in brown, red, and green) — same general technique family as RHC-004 and RHC-012.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
Undated; no signature found in a first-pass review of the visible scene (engraving covers nearly the full face, leaving little blank margin to check).
Mount / Display Stand
Mounted for display on a round, lighter-toned turned wood base via a short peg at the root end — a mount style distinguished from RHC-005 through RHC-012 by its lighter, less heavily finished wood tone.
Color / Technique
Polychrome scrimshaw: black ink line engraving with hand-applied color washes (brown, red, green) — same technique family as RHC-004 (thimble) and RHC-012 (matched pair).
Condition Notes
Root end shows a rough, natural-looking textured surface consistent with other tooth roots in the collection; overall golden-tan surface tone is uniform and could be natural material color, aging, or staining. No visible cracks or losses. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Woodland/wildlife subject (squirrels among flowers) distinct from the marine/nautical themes dominating the collection so far, though consistent with the wildlife vignette tradition seen in RHC-004 (elk), RHC-005 (bison), and RHC-006 (wolf). Deeper overall coloration and lighter wood base mount are both worth comparing against other items once more of the collection is catalogued.
