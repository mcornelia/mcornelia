RHC-015 reviewed

Engraved Whaling Scene on Fossil Ivory Plaque, A.D.A. Studios (Alaska), Serial GD697112, on Wood Easel Stand

Object Type
Engraved flat rectangular plaque (rounded corners), displayed upright on a small wood easel-style stand
Material
Fossil ivory from Alaska — confirmed by a printed label on the reverse reading 'A.D.A. Studios / Fossil Ivory from ALASKA,' alongside a printed serial number 'GD697112.' This is a manufacturer-labeled piece, not a presumed material like most of the collection.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 7.0cm — Width (~7.0cm) estimated off the cm scale; height not measured — ruler in these photos only runs horizontally across the plaque's width.
Subject Matter
A whaling scene: a full-rigged sailing ship at upper left above the horizon line, and a large sperm whale (shown broadside, with visible flukes) dominating the lower two-thirds of the composition, swimming just below the waterline. Rendered in black ink line work with fine cross-hatched shading, no color.
Inscriptions
  • “A.D.A. Studios / Fossil Ivory from ALASKA” (printed label affixed to the reverse (blank) face)
    Manufacturer/studio label, not a hand signature on the engraved face itself.
  • “GD697112” (printed label affixed to the reverse (blank) face, below the studio label)
    Appears to be a serial or batch/lot number, useful for provenance research on the source material or studio records.
Artist Attribution
A.D.A. Studios (manufacturer/studio mark; individual engraver not identified)
Date / Period
Undated on the piece itself; the printed (not hand-engraved) label format suggests a modern production piece rather than a hand-signed antique, though the fossil ivory material itself is ancient.
Mount / Display Stand
Displayed upright in a small dark wood easel-style stand with a felt- or fabric-lined slot that grips the bottom edge of the plaque — a stand style not seen elsewhere in the collection so far (distinct from the peg/rod-and-base mounts used for the tooth-form pieces).
Color / Technique
Traditional black ink line scrimshaw-style engraving with cross-hatched shading, no color
Condition Notes
Surface appears clean and intact with minimal natural mottling compared to the older tooth pieces, consistent with the fossil ivory material and/or more recent manufacture. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First piece in the collection with an explicit manufacturer label and traceable serial number ('A.D.A. Studios,' 'GD697112'), and confirmed fossil ivory material rather than presumed sperm whale tooth. Flat rectangular plaque form and easel stand are both unique to this item so far.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Fossil (mammoth/mastodon) ivory is generally exempt from MMPA/ESA/CITES restrictions that apply to modern whale or elephant ivory, since it comes from extinct species recovered from permafrost/tundra deposits — but state-level rules can vary, so still worth a legal check before any loan/sale; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
Produced by A.D.A. Studios; underlying image likely remains under the studio's copyright, independent of physical ownership.
