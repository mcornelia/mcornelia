Engraved Tall Ship and Breaching Orcas on Rectangular Ivory Plaque

Object Type
Engraved flat rectangular plaque (rounded corners, bordered panel design) — same general form as RHC-015
Material
Unlabeled — no manufacturer mark found on the reverse (checked closely, unlike RHC-015). Given the matching flat rectangular plaque form, rounded corners, and similar bordered-panel engraving style to RHC-015 (confirmed fossil ivory from A.D.A. Studios), this piece is presumed to be the same or a similar fossil ivory material, but this is a stylistic inference, not a confirmed material ID.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 8.0cm — Width (~8.0cm) estimated off the cm scale; height not measured — ruler in these photos only runs horizontally across the plaque's width.
Subject Matter
A full-rigged tall ship under sail at left, and two breaching orcas (killer whales, identifiable by their black-and-white patterning and tall dorsal fins) leaping from the water at right, rendered in black ink line work with fine cross-hatched shading, no color. Same general two-vignette composition style as RHC-015 (ship + marine life) but with orcas rather than a sperm whale.
Inscriptions
  • “Peter Kurczaj (best-effort reading, uncertain spelling of surname)” (engraved on the side edge of the plaque, not the front or back face)
    Cursive signature found on close inspection of the side edge — easy to miss since it's not on either flat face. First name 'Peter' is clearly legible; surname is less certain, read tentatively as 'Kurczaj'.
Artist Attribution
Peter Kurczaj (tentative reading)
Date / Period
Undated; signed on the side edge but no year visible.
Mount / Display Stand
Displayed upright in a small dark wood easel-style stand, the same style used for RHC-015.
Color / Technique
Traditional black ink line scrimshaw-style engraving with cross-hatched shading, no color
Condition Notes
A few small natural-looking marks/chips are visible near the edges of the reverse face; surface otherwise appears clean and intact, similar in tone/finish to RHC-015. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Near-identical plaque form, bordered panel design, and easel-stand mount to RHC-015, strongly suggesting a common source or maker. Unlike RHC-015, this piece carries a hand signature ('Peter Kurczaj,' tentative) engraved on the side edge rather than a printed studio label on the back — worth comparing directly against RHC-015 in person to see if 'Peter Kurczaj' is an engraver working for/with A.D.A. Studios. Orca subject matter is new to the collection.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material unconfirmed — if fossil ivory (as presumed by comparison to RHC-015), likely exempt from MMPA/ESA/CITES restrictions, but this is not confirmed. Flag for legal review before any loan/sale; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
Signed 'Peter Kurczaj' (tentative reading) — underlying image likely remains under the artist's copyright, independent of physical ownership.
