RHC-039 reviewed

Cigarette Holder with Carved Floral (Rose) Grip

Object Type
Cigarette holder (per T. M. Cornelia's identification) — a flattened, elongated holder body with a hollow bore at one end (visible in RHC-039-b, RHC-039-c) into which a cigarette would be inserted, and a grip section carved in high relief with a rose/floral motif.
Material
Presumed ivory or bone; the bore's darker orange-tan interior coloring (RHC-039-b, RHC-039-c) is likely tobacco/tar staining from use rather than a natural material feature, consistent with a functional cigarette holder.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 17.0cm — Overall length (~17.0cm) estimated off the cm scale shown in RHC-039-b.
Subject Matter
A dense high-relief carving of roses and foliage covering the grip section, rather than a figural/portrait bust as in RHC-037 and RHC-038; the holder body is plain and undecorated.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: 19th-early 20th c. floral-carved vanity implement)
Mount / Display Stand
None — unmounted, freestanding object.
Color / Technique
Deep relief floral carving on the grip section only; natural material coloring, with a notably darker orange-tan staining visible inside the hollow bore versus the cream-white exterior, likely from tobacco use. No ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Holder body and grip carving appear intact; the hollow bore shows staining consistent with use. Not physically examined. (Note: RHC-039-a.jpg was captured fully black/blank — likely a lighting or shutter error — and was not usable for cataloging.)
Distinguishing Features
First cigarette holder in the collection, and the first piece with purely floral (non-figural) relief carving rather than a figural bust as in RHC-037/RHC-038 — a functional smoking accessory rather than a letter opener or shoehorn.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory or tooth, species and age would need confirmation before any loan/sale. Flag for legal review and material testing; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No attribution identified; copyright status undetermined.
