RHC-035 reviewed

Turned Ivory-Handled Fountain/Dip Pen with Screw-On Protective Cap

Object Type
Writing instrument — a turned ivory/bone pen body fitted with a metal nib (per Mike Cornelia's identification, a fountain or dip pen nib rather than a sewing stiletto), with external screw threads near the nib that mate to a separate turned cap, which screws on to protect the nib when not in use.
Material
Presumed ivory or bone for both the handle and cap (cream-white, glossy, with ring-turned decorative grooves); steel tip.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 11.0cm — Pen body with exposed nib ~11.0cm; separate cap ~4.0cm; estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos.
Subject Matter
Not applicable — a plain turned utilitarian pen body with ring/groove decorative turning at both ends, no figural or narrative decoration.
Artist Attribution
not applicable
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: 19th-early 20th c. dip/fountain pen)
Mount / Display Stand
None — unmounted, two-piece pen (body + separate screw-on cap), photographed both assembled (RHC-035-a) and disassembled (RHC-035-b, RHC-035-c) with the cap removed to show the exposed nib.
Color / Technique
Plain turned decorative rings/grooves at each end of the handle and matching grooves on the cap; natural material coloring only, no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Pen body, cap, and nib all appear intact and functional (cap threads onto the body cleanly). Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First writing instrument in the collection — the screw-on cap is a practical travel/protective feature not seen on any other implement so far (compare to the uncapped awl/stiletto in RHC-034).
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory, age/species would need confirmation before any loan/sale. Flag for legal review; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
Utilitarian object; no attribution or copyright concern anticipated.
