Carved Diving Eagle Sculpture, Wings Spread, on Oval Wood Base

Object Type
Fully carved (not flat-engraved) three-dimensional sculpture of a diving/landing eagle with fully spread wings, mounted via a metal pin into an oval wood base.
Material
Uncertain — cream-tan coloring is consistent with ivory (as in RHC-027, RHC-028), but unlike those two pieces, no raw/unfinished surface is visible here to confirm genuine tooth/tusk material versus a cast resin or composite reproduction. The very fine, uniform, repetitive feather texture is consistent with either careful hand-carving or mold-casting — not confidently distinguished from photos alone.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 11.0cm — Wingspan (~11.0cm) estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos; the oval wood base itself is smaller than the wingspan, which overhangs it on both sides.
Subject Matter
An eagle in a diving or landing pose, wings fully spread and raised, talons extended downward toward the base, head turned with open beak. Every feather is individually carved with fine striated texture, a much higher level of sculptural detail than RHC-027 or RHC-028.

unknown

undated; no signature or mark found
Mount / Display Stand
Mounted on an oval wood base via a thin metal pin inserted at the talons.
Color / Technique
Fully carved sculptural form with fine individually-carved feather detail; natural/uniform cream-tan material coloring only, no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Surface appears clean and intact with no visible damage to the fine wing/feather carving. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Third fully three-dimensional carved sculpture in the collection (after RHC-027, RHC-028), and by far the most technically elaborate — the fine, uniform feather carving raises a genuine open question about hand-carved versus cast/molded manufacture that photos alone can't resolve, unlike RHC-027/028 where a raw material surface was visible to confirm authenticity.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory, age/species and MMPA/CITES status would need confirmation before any loan/sale; if resin/composite, no wildlife-material restrictions would apply. Flag for legal review and material testing; not a legal determination.
