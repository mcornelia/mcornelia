RHC-011 reviewed

Whaling Scene Engraved Tooth (Ship and Whaleboat) on Turned Wood Base

Object Type
Engraved tooth, mounted on a turned wood display base via a short dark rod/pin
Material
Sperm whale tooth (presumed — conical curved form, cream-to-tan coloration with natural mottling near the root, consistent with other presumed sperm whale teeth in this collection). Base is a dark-stained turned wood puck with a lighter inlaid disk collar where the rod meets the base.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 8.0cm — Tooth length (~8.0cm) estimated off the cm scale (bottom row of the dual-scale ruler); width and base dimensions not separately measured — ruler only runs along one axis in these photos.
Subject Matter
A whaling scene: a full-rigged sailing ship at left, and at right a whaleboat with several crew members (one standing, harpoon/lance raised) beside a diving sperm whale shown mid-sound with its flukes (tail) raised out of the water — a classic 'whaling scene' scrimshaw motif, distinct from the calmer 'ship portrait' seen on RHC-007. Rendered in black ink line work with cross-hatched shading.
Inscriptions
  • “illegible” (along the tan-mottled root edge, lower right of the front/engraved face, beneath the wave line-work near the whaleboat scene)
    A cursive mark consistent with a signature is visible in both wide shots (RHC-011-b) and a macro close-up (RHC-011-d) provided by Mike, but it could not be deciphered at any zoom level attempted — ink has faded into the natural tan staining and/or the shot is slightly motion-blurred at macro range. Believed to be a genuine signature, not confirmed legible.
Artist Attribution
unknown (signature present but illegible)
Date / Period
Undated; a signature is present but illegible after close review of wide and macro photos.
Mount / Display Stand
Mounted for display on a turned, dark-stained wood base with a lighter inlaid disk collar, via a short dark rod/pin at the tooth's root end — similar in general style to RHC-007/RHC-008 but with a distinct two-tone turned base rather than a plain oval or grooved-rim puck.
Color / Technique
Traditional black ink line scrimshaw engraving with cross-hatched shading
Condition Notes
Surface shows natural mottling/tan staining concentrated near the root; no visible cracks or losses in the engraved face. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Full narrative whaling scene (ship + whaleboat + diving whale) rather than a single-subject 'ship portrait' or wildlife vignette — one of the more elaborate, multi-element compositions in the collection so far. Two-tone turned wood base is a mount style not seen on earlier items.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Tooth material presumed sperm whale (same flag as RHC-001) — undated, so pre/post-1972 MMPA status can't be determined from the piece itself. Flag for legal review before any loan/sale; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
Signature present but illegible; copyright status undetermined pending identification.
