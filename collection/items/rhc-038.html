Carved Figural Shoehorn, Egyptian Revival Figure Handle

Object Type
Shoehorn — per Mike Cornelia's identification, the broad, flat, curved/scooped blade end (rather than a tapered point) is consistent with a shoehorn rather than a letter opener/page turner (contrast RHC-037) — with a carved figural handle depicting an Egyptian-style profile head and beaded collar.
Material
Presumed ivory (smooth cream-white surface, fine carving detail); species not confirmed.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 23.0cm — Overall length (~23.0cm) estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos — slightly longer than RHC-037.
Subject Matter
A stylized profile head wearing a tall crown or headdress element and a broad beaded collar (Egyptian 'wesekh'-style collar), carved in the Egyptian Revival tradition — a decorative style that surged in popularity in the West following the 1922 discovery of Tutankhamun's tomb. The handle transitions from the figural bust into a fluted/draped collar motif before meeting the broad, curved shoehorn blade.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: early-mid 20th c. Egyptian Revival, likely post-1922)
Mount / Display Stand
None — unmounted, freestanding shoehorn.
Color / Technique
Fully carved figural handle with fine headdress and beaded-collar detail, transitioning to a plain, broad, curved shoehorn blade; natural material coloring only, no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Blade and figural handle appear intact with no visible chips or cracks. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First shoehorn in the collection, and the first piece in the Egyptian Revival decorative tradition — a notable outlier in origin/style compared to the whaling and Arctic-themed majority of the collection. Worth comparing the figural carving style directly against RHC-037's East Asian export letter opener, even though the two objects serve different functions.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Material not confirmed. If genuine ivory, age/species and import history would need confirmation before any loan/sale (CITES/ESA considerations for non-domestic ivory). Flag for legal review; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No attribution identified; copyright status undetermined.
