Matched Pair of Carved Ivory Hairpins with Elephant and Openwork Ball Finials

Object Type
A matched pair of hairpins (or hatpins), each with a long tapering pointed shaft and an elaborate finial consisting of a pierced/openwork lattice ball surmounted by a small carved standing elephant figure.
Material
Presumed ivory (fine, dense carving detail, cream-white color) — consistent with Chinese or South/Southeast Asian export carved ivory hairpin traditions.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 10.0cm — Each pin ~10.0cm long, estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos; sold/kept as a matched pair.
Subject Matter
A small standing elephant figure atop each finial, perched on a pierced, lattice-carved spherical 'puzzle ball' style openwork bead, above a fluted collar transitioning into the long tapering pin shaft.
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: Chinese or South/Southeast Asian export carved ivory hairpins)

None — unmounted, freestanding pair.
Color / Technique
Fine openwork/pierced lattice carving on the ball finials with a small fully carved elephant atop each; natural material coloring only, no ink work or paint.
Condition Notes
Both pins appear intact with fine, delicate openwork carving preserved; shafts appear straight and undamaged. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First hairpins/hatpins in the collection, and the first pieces to combine openwork/pierced 'puzzle ball' carving with figural elephant elements — fits within the emerging East/South Asian export sub-group alongside RHC-037 (letter opener) and RHC-040 (dragon cigarette holder), consistent with the noted upcoming Asian-influenced material (including netsuke).
