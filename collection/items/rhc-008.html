RHC-008 reviewed

Bass Harbor Light Engraved Tooth on Turned Wood Base

Object Type
Engraved tooth, mounted on a turned wood display base with a felt-covered bottom
Material
Sperm whale tooth (presumed, same reasoning as RHC-001/007 — conical curved form, dense surface with age mottling; the root end is naturally rough/nodular in texture, visible in RHC-008-c, consistent with an unpolished natural tooth root). Base is a dark-stained turned wood puck with a decorative grooved rim, felt-covered on the underside.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 6.8cm — Tooth length (~6.8cm) measured off the cm scale; width and base dimensions not separately measured — ruler only runs along one axis in these photos.
Subject Matter
Bass Harbor Head Light — a lighthouse and adjoining keeper's house scene, with pine trees and a rocky/hilly coastal setting, rendered in black ink line work with cross-hatched shading. Identified by a handwritten label on the base's felt bottom reading 'BASS HARBOR LIGHT' (RHC-008-d) — a well-known lighthouse in Acadia National Park, Mount Desert Island, Maine.
Inscriptions
  • “BASS HARBOR LIGHT” (handwritten on a paper label affixed to the felt-covered underside of the wood base)
    identifies the subject matter; not a signature on the tooth itself
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
Undated; no artist signature found. A faint area of shading near the top of the scene was checked at high zoom and does not appear to be legible text.
Mount / Display Stand
Mounted for display on a turned, dark-stained wood base with a decorative grooved rim and felt-covered bottom (fanciest/most finished base style seen so far in this batch) via a small light-colored peg where the tooth's root meets the base.
Color / Technique
Traditional black ink line scrimshaw engraving with cross-hatched shading
Condition Notes
Engraved face appears intact with natural age mottling; root end has a rough, nodular natural texture (not damage). Wood base and felt bottom appear in good condition. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
Named/identified subject (Bass Harbor Head Light, Maine) via a handwritten label on the base — the clearest identification of a specific real-world location in the collection so far, useful for cataloging and possibly for dating/provenance research.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Tooth material presumed sperm whale (same flag as RHC-001) — undated, so pre/post-1972 MMPA status can't be determined from the piece itself. Flag for legal review before any loan/sale; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No artist signature identified; copyright status undetermined.

Open Research Questions

Research finding (phase 3, 2026-07-15): Bass Harbor Head Light (Mount Desert Island, Maine) is a well-documented, frequently photographed and painted lighthouse — one of the most reproduced lighthouse images in Maine tourism/postcard material historically, which supports the theory that this engraving was copied from a widely circulated reference photo/postcard rather than sketched from life. No specific source photo was pinpointed via general web search; a side-by-side comparison against period Bass Harbor postcards would be the way to narrow further. Handwriting on the label was not assessed (would require direct physical/high-res comparison against samples of Richard Cornelia's handwriting).
