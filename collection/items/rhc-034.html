Two-Piece Ivory-Handled Manicure/Grooming Set: Tweezers and Turned Awl/Stiletto

Object Type
A pair of small handled implements, likely a manicure or sewing/grooming set: (1) a smooth tapered cream handle fitted with metal tweezers — shown closed (appearing as a single flat blade) in RHC-034-a and RHC-034-c, and open (arms spread, per Mike Cornelia's identification) in RHC-034-b; and (2) a shorter handle with a spiral-carved/ribbed grip, fitted with a thin pointed steel spike (awl or stiletto), consistent across all photos.
Material
Presumed ivory or bone for both handles (cream-white, glossy); metal working ends are steel (file/awl) and a brass or gold-toned metal ferrule joins the handle to the metal on the file/hook tool.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 13.0cm — Longer tool (tweezers) ~13.0cm, shorter tool (awl/stiletto) ~11.5cm, estimated off the cm scale shown in the photos.
Subject Matter
Not applicable — plain turned/carved handles with no figural or narrative decoration; the shorter handle has a spiral-ribbed turned form, the longer handle is a smooth tapered form.
Artist Attribution
not applicable
Date / Period
undated (stylistic estimate: 19th-early 20th c. manicure/sewing implement set)
Mount / Display Stand
None — unmounted, freestanding tools.
Color / Technique
Plain turned/carved handles, no engraving, ink work, or paint; natural material coloring only.
Condition Notes
Both handles appear intact and glossy; metal ends show typical tarnish/oxidation. The tweezers appear functional, opening and closing cleanly across the two photo angles. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First manicure/sewing implement set in the collection combining tweezers with a separate turned-handle awl/stiletto; the spiral-ribbed handle on the awl is a distinctive decorative turning not seen elsewhere so far.
