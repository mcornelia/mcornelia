RHC-018 reviewed

"Empty Handed" — Engraved Whaleboat Scene Panel Set in Decorative Wood Frame, Dated 1999

Object Type
Engraved flat oval/rounded panel inset into a shaped, scalloped-edge wood display frame with a black inner border
Material
Panel material not confirmed (presumed ivory or bone based on cream-white color and fine grain, consistent with other engraved panels in the collection); no direct view of the panel's reverse was available since it is permanently set into the wood frame. Frame is a reddish-brown hardwood (rosewood or similar) with a scalloped/shaped outline and a black-painted or lacquered inner border around the panel.
Dimensions (cm)
length: 12.0cm — Overall wood frame width (~12.0cm) estimated off the cm scale; the inset engraved panel itself is smaller (roughly 8cm across, not separately measured). Height not measured — ruler only runs horizontally in these photos.
Subject Matter
A dramatic whaling scene: a full-rigged ship in the distance at left, and in the foreground a whaleboat crowded with crew members straining at the oars/lines amid rough, whitecapped seas — a more dynamic, close-up 'in the boat' perspective than the calmer ship-and-whale compositions seen elsewhere (e.g. RHC-011, RHC-015). Rendered in fine black ink line work with detailed cross-hatched shading, no color. The title 'Empty Handed' (engraved on a small metal plate on the frame) suggests this depicts an unsuccessful hunt — the boat returning without a catch.
Inscriptions
  • “Empty Handed” (engraved on a small oval metal plate affixed to the wood frame's edge)
    This is the piece's title, not an artist signature.
  • “1999” (engraved on a round wood medallion inset into the back/reverse of the wood frame)
    Clear date, giving this piece a firm year — the first precisely dated item in the collection besides RHC-001 (1990).
Artist Attribution
unknown
Date / Period
1999 (dated via medallion on the reverse of the frame)
Mount / Display Stand
The engraved panel is permanently set into a decorative, scalloped-edge hardwood frame with a black inner border — a fundamentally different presentation style from the peg/rod-mounted teeth and easel-stand plaques seen elsewhere in the collection; this is a finished wall/tabletop display piece in its own right rather than a mounted specimen.
Color / Technique
Traditional black ink line scrimshaw engraving with fine cross-hatched shading, no color
Condition Notes
Panel and frame both appear to be in excellent, clean condition with no visible damage, consistent with the piece's more recent date (1999) and finished/framed presentation. Not physically examined.
Distinguishing Features
First piece in the collection with both a titled inscription ('Empty Handed') and a firm inset date (1999); also the first piece presented in a finished decorative frame rather than a rod, peg, or easel mount. The 'in the boat' whaling perspective (crew straining at oars in rough seas) is a more narratively dramatic composition than the distanced ship-and-whale views elsewhere.
Legal / Regulatory Status
Panel material and species not confirmed — flag for legal review before any loan/sale once material is identified. Piece postdates the 1972 MMPA cutoff (dated 1999), so if the panel is any regulated marine mammal ivory, modern-manufacture restrictions would likely apply; not a legal determination.
Rights / Copyright
No artist signature identified; copyright status undetermined. Titled 'Empty Handed,' which may aid future identification of the artist or publisher.
